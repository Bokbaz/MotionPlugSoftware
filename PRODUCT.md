# Motion Plug Product Context

## Register

product

## Users

Motion Plug is for video editors working inside Adobe Premiere Pro, usually in a narrow docked panel while an active sequence occupies most of the screen. They need to find a polished motion treatment quickly, preview it accurately, adapt only the controls that matter, and place it at the playhead without breaking their edit. The primary workflow is choose, customize, optionally add sound, then insert or update.

## Product Purpose

Motion Plug is an offline motion-graphics tool for browsing, previewing, customizing, inserting, and revising original animated titles and information graphics. It should make a high-quality result faster than leaving Premiere to build the treatment manually. Success means that the panel preview is trustworthy, the timeline action is explicit and safe, controls remain easy to revisit, and errors explain what the editor can do next.

## Brand Personality

Confident, precise, calm. The product should feel thoughtfully made and editor-native: restrained enough to disappear into a professional workflow, but distinctive enough that its original presets feel authored rather than generic.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic slideshow-template styling, ornamental dashboard cards, neon-on-black “creative tool” clichés, glass effects, excessive gradients, bouncy motion, busy backgrounds, crowded controls, and interfaces that imitate Apple branding instead of learning from its clarity and timing. Do not imply unsupported behaviors such as automatic footage tracking, native timeline drag-and-drop, or exact host rendering when a preview is only approximate.

## Design Principles

1. Make the animation visible before asking for configuration.
2. Keep the core path linear: choose, customize, add.
3. Earn trust with exact previews, honest host status, and actionable errors.
4. Preserve editing context with compact controls, stable actions, and no nested scrolling.
5. Let strong typography and deliberate timing carry the personality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast where the host permits it. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, clear accessible names, reduced-motion preferences, non-color-only status cues, readable text at narrow panel widths, and resilient handling of long text, manual line breaks, punctuation, and accented characters.
